Description:
Full project walkthrough video for the Automatic Street Light engineering project. The recording covers the complete process: circuit assembly on the breadboard, TinkerCAD simulation under different lighting conditions, the live breadboard test (LED switching ON in darkness and OFF in light), and a guided walk-through of the troubleshooting cases where component values were intentionally changed to demonstrate failure modes.

Both implementations are featured in the same video:
- The analog transistor design (Project 1)
- The Arduino-based design (Project 2)

The video is hosted on Google Drive (link above). For the underlying reports, calculations and schematics, see entry 10 (Automatic Street Light — Engineering Project).
